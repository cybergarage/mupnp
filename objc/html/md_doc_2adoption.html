mUPnP for Objective-C
Adoption evidence

Sources rechecked: 2026-10-22. All PDF page numbers below are 1-based, counted from the beginning of the PDF, including covers, rather than inferred printed page numbers. Accessible sources were read, not just search snippets.

mUPnP for C (formerly CyberLink for C) is one continuous project. The ChangeLog records its rename in 3.0.0 on 2015-09-02; historical CyberLink for C and clinkc references are part of its adoption history. The names and versions below reproduce the evidence rather than dividing that history into separate projects. A source naming 2.x does not establish use of the latest 3.x release. No partnership, endorsement, sales volume or particular implementation role is inferred.

llink
